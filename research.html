01 / FUNDAMENTAL MECHANISMS

Fundamental Electrochemical Mechanisms

Exploring how battery materials transform during operation, with particular attention to coupled ion transport, phase transitions, dissolution–deposition reactions and electrochemical kinetics. Operando X-ray techniques help connect structural changes to measured performance.

Mn-PBA · MnO₂ · Operando XRD · XAS · Reaction pathways

Reaction Mechanism of Mn-Based Prussian Blue Analogues

Investigating the electrochemical transformation of Mn-based Prussian blue analogues in aqueous zinc electrolytes. The study combines electrochemical analysis, operando characterisation and mechanistic interpretation to distinguish intercalation from conversion and dissolution–redeposition processes. The results highlight the influence of electrolyte composition, structural evolution and manganese chemistry on reversible capacity and cycling behaviour.

Electrochemical Mechanisms of MnO₂ Cathodes

Examining the contributions of manganese dissolution, deposition and surface passivation to the cycling response of aqueous zinc–manganese dioxide batteries. Electrochemical and operando observations help explain how reaction conditions affect capacity and structural evolution, offering mechanistic insights for more reliable cathode design.

02 / INTERFACES & SCALE-UP

Battery Interfaces, Mechanics & Scale-Up

Investigating how external pressure, evolving interfaces and cell architecture influence battery performance. Work on constant-volume and constant-pressure fixtures connects zinc-anode behaviour with practical symmetric and full-cell pouch configurations.

Zn anodes · Stack pressure · Pouch cells · X-ray CT · Neutron imaging

Mechanical Constraints on Zinc-Anode Cycling

Comparing constant-volume and constant-pressure pouch-cell configurations to understand the role of mechanical boundary conditions in zinc deposition, stripping and interfacial stability. Electrochemical measurements, pressure sensing and imaging provide complementary information about evolving cell behaviour and failure processes.

Ongoing research

Pressure Effects in Full-Cell Pouch Batteries

Extending mechanical investigations to aqueous zinc pouch cells with different cathode chemistries, including MnO₂, iodine and zinc vanadium oxide. The work examines the interactions among stack pressure, gas formation, interfacial evolution and cycling performance to identify design considerations for practical cells.

03 / COMPUTATIONAL SCIENCE

Computational Materials Science

Using first-principles calculations and atomistic simulations to investigate structural stability, reaction energetics, electronic structure and interfacial processes. Computational insights complement experiments by testing plausible mechanisms at the atomic scale.

DFT · AIMD · Electronic structure · Energetics · Interfaces

First-Principles Modelling of Functional Materials

Applying density functional theory and molecular dynamics to examine atomic structure, bonding, electronic properties and thermodynamic stability. Calculations of reaction energies and local atomic environments support mechanistic interpretation and guide comparisons between candidate materials and interfaces.
